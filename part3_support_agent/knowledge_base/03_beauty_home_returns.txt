Beauty products may be returned within 10 days of delivery if they are unopened, unused, and in their original sealed packaging.

Opened or used beauty products are generally not eligible for return for hygiene and safety reasons, unless the product was defective, damaged during delivery, or incorrect.

Home products may be returned within 15 days of delivery if they are unused and returned with their original packaging and included accessories.

Home products that are damaged due to customer misuse, improper installation, or missing components may not be eligible for return.
