For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, approved refunds are processed after the returned product passes the required quality check.

Customers may be asked to provide valid bank account or supported payment details so the refund can be transferred electronically.

Refund processing begins after return verification, and the time required for the refunded amount to appear may depend on the customer's bank or payment provider.

If the returned item fails the quality check or does not satisfy the applicable return policy, the refund may be declined.
