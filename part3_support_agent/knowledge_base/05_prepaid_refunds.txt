For prepaid orders, approved refunds are generally returned to the original payment method used when the order was placed.

Refund processing begins after the returned product is received and successfully passes the applicable quality check.

After the refund is initiated, the time required for the amount to appear in the customer's account may vary depending on the bank, card issuer, UPI provider, or wallet provider.

If a refund cannot be processed to the original payment method, customer support may request an alternative eligible refund method.
