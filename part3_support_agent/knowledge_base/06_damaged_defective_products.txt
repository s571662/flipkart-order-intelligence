Customers who receive a damaged or defective product should report the issue within the return period applicable to that product category.

The customer may be asked to provide photographs or other evidence showing the damage or defect before the return or replacement request is approved.

Products confirmed to have been damaged during delivery or received with a manufacturing defect may qualify for a replacement or refund after verification.

Damage caused by customer misuse, accidental damage after delivery, unauthorized modification, or improper installation may not qualify for a return or replacement.
