If a customer receives the wrong product or an item is missing from the delivered package, the issue should be reported to customer support within the applicable return or replacement period.

Customers may be asked to provide photographs of the received package, shipping label, product, and packaging to help verify the issue.

After verification, an incorrectly delivered product may qualify for replacement or return, while a confirmed missing item may qualify for replacement or refund.

Requests may be declined if the available evidence indicates that the correct and complete order was successfully delivered.
