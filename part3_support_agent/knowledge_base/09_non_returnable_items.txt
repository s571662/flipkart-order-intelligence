Certain products may be marked as non-returnable because of hygiene, safety, customization, or product-specific restrictions.

Items identified as non-returnable on the product or order policy are generally not eligible for return due to a change of mind.

If a non-returnable product is received damaged, defective, incorrect, or incomplete, the customer should contact support because the issue may still qualify for verification and resolution.

Return eligibility is determined according to the policy applicable to the specific product and the reason for the return request.
