Customers may cancel an order before it has been shipped, provided the cancellation option is still available for that order.

Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may no longer be available and the customer may need to follow the applicable return process after delivery.

For prepaid orders that are successfully cancelled, the refund will be initiated to the original payment method.

Cancellation availability may depend on the order status, product type, and fulfillment stage at the time the cancellation request is submitted.
