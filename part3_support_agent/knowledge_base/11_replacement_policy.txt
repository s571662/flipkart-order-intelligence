A product may qualify for replacement if it is received damaged, defective, incorrect, or incomplete and the issue is reported within the applicable return or replacement period.

Replacement requests may require verification of the product condition, accessories, packaging, and supporting evidence such as photographs.

If the requested replacement item is unavailable, the customer may be offered a refund or another eligible resolution according to the applicable policy.

Products damaged through customer misuse, unauthorized modification, or improper handling after delivery may not qualify for replacement.
