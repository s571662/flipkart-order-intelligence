Approved refunds are initiated after the returned product is received and successfully passes the applicable quality and eligibility checks.

The time required for the refunded amount to appear in the customer's account may vary depending on the original payment method, bank, card issuer, UPI provider, or wallet provider.

Customers should allow the normal processing period after refund initiation before reporting a delayed refund.

If a refund has been initiated but is not received within the expected processing period, the customer should contact support with the relevant order and refund details for further investigation.
