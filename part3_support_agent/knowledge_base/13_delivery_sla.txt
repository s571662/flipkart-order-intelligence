Standard delivery orders are generally expected to arrive within 3 to 7 business days, depending on the destination, product availability, and fulfillment location.

Express or priority delivery orders may have shorter expected delivery windows when that service is available for the selected product and destination.

If an order is not delivered within its expected delivery window, the customer should check the latest tracking update and contact support if there is no recent movement or confirmed delivery exception.

Delivery timelines are estimates and may be affected by weather, carrier delays, regional restrictions, holidays, or other operational disruptions.
